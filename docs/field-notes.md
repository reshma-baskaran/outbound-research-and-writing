# Field notes from real campaign work

This operating system was distilled from campaign work completed across three recurring situations. Recipient records, private copy, and sending-platform data are intentionally excluded.

## Dormant-lead reactivation

The challenge was not generating another product introduction. It was deciding whether the account was still active, whether the prior use case remained plausible, and what had changed enough to justify restarting the conversation. This produced the `keep / rewrite / suppress / needs_research` decision layer.

## Time-sensitive outreach

Open Enrollment work made timing part of the message architecture. A deadline can create relevance, but it cannot replace account fit. This produced the rule that urgency must be tied to an actual operating window and never manufactured in copy.

## Account-personalized ABM

Retail and financial-services research showed that one signal cannot be stretched across six emails. This produced the account-use-case map, source record, fact-versus-hypothesis boundary, and six distinct touch roles.

These are operating lessons from Reshma Baskaran's campaign work. They are not claims that every campaign used identical tooling or delivered the same outcome.
