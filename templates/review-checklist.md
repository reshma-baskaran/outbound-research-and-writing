# Sequence review checklist

Use this checklist after structural validation and before any send decision.

- [ ] Account-specific claims map to a source URL and access date.
- [ ] Confirmed facts, hypotheses, and unknowns remain distinct.
- [ ] Email 1 states a concrete operating pressure and consequence.
- [ ] Each touch has a different job and does not repeat the prior email.
- [ ] Only Email 1 has a subject.
- [ ] Every CTA is small and answerable.
- [ ] The copy does not narrate the research process.
- [ ] The signoff matches the actual sender.
- [ ] Sender health, audience, suppression, and send controls are reviewed.
- [ ] A human has approved the final draft.
