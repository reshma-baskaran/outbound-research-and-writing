# Quality gate

Check all of the following:

- Email 1 is short and immediately understandable.
- The first two lines could not apply unchanged to every account.
- Account-specific statements have a source in the research map.
- The operating consequence is explicit.
- Each email adds a different reason to care.
- Only Email 1 has a subject.
- Every email begins with `Hi [first name],` for downstream personalization.
- Every CTA is answerable without a large commitment.
- The copy does not mention the research process.
- The copy avoids generic AI-transformation language.
- The signoff exactly matches the campaign's `sender_identity`; it is never
  inherited from the repository author.
- No upload, campaign write, or launch has occurred without approval.

Reject unsupported certainty, invented precision, false familiarity, and claims of customer relationships or results that are not documented.
