# Account-use-case map

Create one row per account with:

- account
- domain
- persona and function
- launch action: `keep`, `rewrite`, `suppress`, or `needs_research`
- action reason
- confirmed account facts
- source URLs and access dates
- operating-pressure hypothesis
- evidence status: `confirmed`, `inferred`, or `unknown`
- mapped workflow or use case
- Email 1 through Email 6 angles
- confidence

## Source order

1. Company newsroom, filings, product, support, careers, and investor pages.
2. Named technology-partner case studies.
3. Credible reporting and conference material.
4. Customer complaints and community discussions, treated as patterns rather than settled fact.
5. Data providers, treated as enrichment rather than unquestioned truth.

## Decision boundary

A source proves only what it says. A hiring post can prove that a role was advertised; it does not prove a funded transformation. A product page can prove a capability is marketed; it does not prove adoption or satisfaction. Record the observation separately from its ABM implication.
